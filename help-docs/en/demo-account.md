# The demo account

Want to try contado with zero commitment? On the sign-in screen, type the word `tester` instead of your email.

## What you get

The full app, already signed in — upload, sales, clients, owed, exports, all of it.

## What to know

- The demo account is **shared and public**. Everyone who types the demo word lands in the same account and sees what you put there.
- **Don't put real numbers in it** — no real client names, no real amounts you'd mind a stranger reading.
- It can't be deleted, and anything you add may be changed or cleared by other people trying the app.

When it feels right, sign in with your own email and start your real books — the free core is free forever.
