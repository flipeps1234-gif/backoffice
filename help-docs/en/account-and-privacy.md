# Your account and your data

## Signing in

Type your email and we send you a sign-in link — no password to remember. Once you're in, every change saves to your account the moment you make it, and it's there on any device you sign into.

## What we keep, what we don't

- The payment **screenshots you upload are read, and contado doesn't store them** — OpenAI reads them and may keep them for up to 30 days to check for abuse.
- A **photo you attach to a sale is kept** with that sale until you delete your account — that's the point of attaching it.
- We never sell your data. There are no ads. We never ask for your bank login.

## Leaving

Your rows are yours: **export every row, free, whenever you want** — before deleting or just because. Photos attached to sales aren't in the export, so save any you need from each sale first.

Deleting your account is in Settings: type your email to confirm, and your ledger is erased for good 7 days later. You can change your mind inside those 7 days by tapping cancel. After the purge there is nothing of your ledger left to recover.

A few things sit outside your ledger: an email you left on the founding list is kept until you ask us to remove it, OpenAI may keep an uploaded screenshot for up to 30 days, upload counts can stay for up to about 36 days without your account attached, and copies in our providers' logs and backups can take longer to expire.
