# Your account and your data

## Signing in

Type your email and we send you a sign-in link — no password to remember. Once you're in, every change saves to your account the moment you make it, and it's there on any device you sign into.

## What we keep, what we don't

- The payment **screenshots you upload are read and discarded** — they are not stored.
- A **photo you attach to a sale is kept** with that sale until you delete your account — that's the point of attaching it.
- We never sell your data. There are no ads. We never ask for your bank login.

## Leaving

Your rows are yours: **export everything, free, whenever you want** — before deleting or just because.

Deleting your account is in Settings: type your email to confirm, and everything is erased for good 7 days later. You can change your mind inside those 7 days by tapping cancel. After the purge there is nothing left to recover.
