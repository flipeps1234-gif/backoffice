# Uploading screenshots

The app reads pictures of your payment feeds and pulls out every payment it can find: who paid, how much, and when.

## What works

- **Venmo**: screenshot your **Transactions tab** — the list with amounts. The social feed doesn't show amounts, so it can't be read; the app will tell you if you upload it.
- **Cash App and Zelle**: the activity list with names and amounts.
- **Receipts, checks, statements**: snap a photo the same way — money out is read just like money in.

Upload several screenshots at a time. Reading screenshots has fair-use limits while we build; if you reach one, the app tells you — try again later. If two screenshots overlap, the app looks for repeated payments and keeps one copy; you still check every row.

## What to check

The reading is automatic and it sometimes gets things wrong. Amounts, names and dates are guesses until you confirm them — the app highlights the fields it wasn't sure about, and what ends up in your books is always your call.

Your screenshots are sent to OpenAI to be read. contado keeps the rows, not the images; OpenAI may keep an image for up to 30 days to check for abuse.
