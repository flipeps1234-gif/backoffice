# Getting started

contado turns your payment screenshots into books. You upload pictures, the app reads them, you confirm what it found — that's the whole job.

## The first five minutes

- Sign in with your email and skip or finish the short welcome tour.
- On a phone, tap the box at the top and add screenshots of your Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle payments; on a laptop, choose **Upload screenshots** in the left menu — as many as you like.
- The app reads every payment it can find and shows you a filled-in list. Anything it wasn't sure about is highlighted — tap those and fix them.
- Mark each payment **business or personal** — swipe on a phone (right for business, left for personal), or use the two buttons. Your totals climb as you go.
- Cash job with no screenshot? Log a sale by hand — a couple of taps.

After your first batch you'll already see your period total, your busiest day, and your top payer. That never costs anything.

## Do I need an account?

Yes — the app opens once you sign in. Signing in is just your email — we send you a link, no password. After that, every change saves to your account instantly. Before that, the sorting demo on our home page works without one.
